# Publishing and live data

- Never deploy, push to `main`, or change production data without the user's explicit instruction. A push to `main` triggers a website deployment. Keep review changes local until authorized.

# Menu wording

- Use simple, familiar words in menu copy. Avoid words such as "assorted"; use "a mix of" instead. Keep product and ingredient names the user specifically requests.
- Use natural Lebanese Arabic where appropriate. Keep familiar product names such as "وافل ستيك" and clear Arabic ingredient names such as "شوكولا داكنة". Avoid literal wording such as "تغطية" for a choice of chocolate; prefer "الشوكولا اللي بتختارها".
- For chocolate-covered crêpes, prefer "مغطّى بميكس من الشوكولا" to "وعليه ميكس شوكولا".
- Do not call chocolate or spreads "صوص" or "صلصة" in Arabic menu copy. Use "شوكولا", the spread's name, or "خيارات للتغميس" when the dipping choices are unspecified.
- When the owner confirms an ingredient is used, name it directly (for example, "made with Lotus Biscoff", not "Lotus Biscoff flavor"). Avoid vague phrases such as "a rich flavor".
- Prefer "2 وافل" to "وافلين" and "معمول" to "منعمله". Keep mixed drink descriptions simple ("بارد ومنعش"). Do not use "طازة" for ice cream rolls.
